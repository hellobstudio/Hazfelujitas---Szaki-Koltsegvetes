2. In short

The app does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter or photograph in the app is stored only on your own device. The Publisher has no access to it. The app has no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no crash reporting.

The Publisher processes personal data about you only when:

  • you buy the app on Google Play (limited purchase data provided by Google, see section 4), or
  • you contact the Publisher by email (see section 5).

3. Data you enter in the app

The app stores what you enter: renovation phases, budgets, quotes, payments, ratings, contractors' names and contact details, your own (client) details, and the photos and documents you add.

  • Where is it stored? In the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to any server.
  • Who can access it? Only you. The Publisher cannot.
  • Other people's data: if you record other people's details in the app (for example, contractors), you process them for your own purposes. For private individuals this is usually a purely personal or household activity under Article 2(2)(c) GDPR. If you use the app for business, you are the controller of that data.
  • Camera and files: to take a photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app or file picker. It does not request camera or storage permissions. It saves only the picture or document you choose.
  • Backup, export, sharing: when you create a backup or a PDF/Excel export, or share a file, the Android share menu opens. You decide where the file goes (for example, Google Drive, email or a messenger). From then on, that service's privacy terms apply.
  • Android Auto Backup: if Android backup is enabled in your Google account, the system may back up the app's data to your Google account. Google does this under your account settings. The Publisher cannot access this data either.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings → Apps → Renovation → Storage → Clear data.

4. Purchases on Google Play

Purchases and payments are handled by Google (Google Commerce Limited / Google Ireland Limited, Ireland). Google processes your purchase data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

For accounting purposes, Google provides the Publisher with limited sales data. This can include the order number, date, product, price, currency, country, and in some cases the region or postal code. The Publisher does not receive your name, email address or payment details.

  • Purpose: bookkeeping, tax returns, handling refunds.
  • Legal basis: compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR; Hungarian accounting and tax laws).
  • Retention: as required by accounting and tax law, up to 8 years.
  • Recipients: the Publisher's accountant and, where required by law, the tax authority.

5. If you email the Publisher

If you contact the Publisher, they process your email address and the content of your message. This includes anything else you choose to include, such as your name, phone model or app version.

  • Purpose: answering your request, fixing bugs, handling complaints.
  • Legal basis: the Publisher's legitimate interest in supporting users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For complaints, compliance with consumer protection law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Retention: 1 year after the request is closed. Complaints are kept as long as consumer protection law requires (at least 5 years).
  • Recipients: the Publisher's email provider. The Publisher does not share your data with anyone else unless required by law.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

The Publisher does not transfer the data they process outside the European Economic Area. Google and the email provider operate under their own terms and use appropriate safeguards, such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights

Regarding the data the Publisher holds about you, you have the right to:

  • have it corrected (Art. 16),
  • have it erased (Art. 17),
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • receive it in a portable format, where applicable (Art. 20).

Send your request to monormatika@gmail.com. The Publisher will reply within one month. This can be extended by two further months where necessary, in which case you will be informed.

The Publisher cannot access, provide or delete the data stored inside the app on your device. You manage that data directly in the app.
